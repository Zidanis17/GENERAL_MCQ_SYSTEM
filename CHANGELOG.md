# MCQ Studio release notes

## [1.3.0]

- Choose Light, Dark, or System appearance across the app.
- Adjust study text with Standard, Large, and Extra large sizes.
- Save your preferred study mode, shuffle settings, and exam timer.
- Combine multiple banks into named saved mixes with a question count from each bank.
- Draw fresh questions every session from frozen bank copies, even after the originals are replaced or deleted.
- See question sources during sessions and reviews; resume and retry mixed exams with their saved questions intact.

Existing question banks, results, and unfinished sessions are preserved when upgrading.
